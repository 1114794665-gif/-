How to choose custom shot glasses

Three checks before you approve a model and artwork.

01 / CAPACITY

Start with the pour

Choose 1.5 oz for tastings and giveaways, or 2 oz for a standard shot. Collapsible sets are reviewed as a set, with the filled size confirmed before artwork is placed.

02 / CUP FORM

Match the serve

Silicone styles suit flexible, unbreakable service; LED acrylic styles suit a lit bar or nightclub. Tell us where the shots will be served and we will shortlist the listed models.

03 / PRINT POSITION

Check the artwork area

Confirm where the logo sits on the cup, then review the decoration method against the selected material. LED styles are checked with the light on and off before the sample is approved.

From your logo to the artwork, we help create a custom look that is unmistakably yours—bringing your brand’s personality to every cup, ice bucket and accessory.

What capacity should I choose for custom shot glasses?

Start with the pour you serve: 1.5 oz suits tastings and branded giveaways, while 2 oz works for a standard shot. For collapsible sets, confirm the filled and folded size before artwork is placed.

Can I print on silicone and LED shot glasses?

Yes, but the method follows the material. Silicone styles are reviewed for pad or screen printing; LED acrylic styles are checked with the light on and off. Send your artwork and we will confirm the usable position on the selected model.

What should I send for a shot glass quote?

Send the model shortlist, quantity, artwork, preferred colours, packaging and destination. If you are still choosing, tell us the drink, venue and event date and we will narrow the options.
