Prepare a clear PET cup enquiry

Record the model, target capacity and estimated quantity for each item you are considering. If you have not chosen a model, describe the shape, intended setting and reference dimensions. Compare the material, lid, straw and included accessories separately: similar photographs do not establish that two configurations have the same specifications.

For PET cups, describe the drink, intended serving temperature, single or repeated use, and proposed cleaning method. Ask us to review suitability for the selected model. Include any lid or straw reference so compatibility can be checked in the quotation and sample. If documentation is required, identify the destination market, requested document and exact model.

For branding, share available artwork, colour references, preferred logo placement and packaging ideas. We will help you confirm the decoration method, usable area and kit contents for the chosen product. Add the destination country, postal code, required arrival date and any split deliveries. Mark undecided requirements clearly; quantities, specifications and delivery arrangements remain subject to the project quotation and sample approval.
