What should be agreed for the cup body, lid and artwork?

Agree your target capacity, open and folded dimensions, cup construction, lid or sleeve, artwork and packing. The customized specification is confirmed in the quotation and sample review.

What should I review on a sample before a bulk order?

Agree the review points and acceptance criteria for the selected model, then check the sample, artwork and packing. These are review areas, not claims that a model has passed a performance test.

Buying Questions

How do I choose a collapsible silicone cup for an outdoor project?

Start with your required serving capacity, carrying space and need for a lid. Choose a reference model and send your target open and folded dimensions; capacity and size are customized and agreed in the quotation.

Can I customize the capacity and folded size of a collapsible cup?

Yes. Contact us with the capacity, open dimensions and folded size you need. We review the reference structure and confirm the agreed specification for your project; a volume in a reference name does not fix the customized order.

Can collapsible cups be decorated with my logo?

Request a review of the artwork size, position and intended folding action on the exact model. Decoration compatibility and the finished appearance should be approved with a sample.

What is the minimum order quantity for custom cups?

The minimum order quantity is 10 pieces. Include the model, artwork, colour, packing and required quantity when requesting a project quotation.

How much do custom cups with a logo cost?

A project quote is needed because the model, quantity, decoration, accessories, packing and delivery affect the price. Compare the same specification and ask which setup, sample, tooling and delivery charges are included. External reference prices are not published here as confirmed selling prices.

Can I request samples before a bulk order?

Yes. Discuss an existing model sample, a decorated sample or a pre-production sample for the selected project. Confirm the sample specification, charges, preparation time, freight and any credit terms in writing. A digital proof checks layout and does not replace physical sample approval.

How are production and delivery dates confirmed?

Dates are confirmed for the agreed model, quantity, artwork, sample approval and delivery destination. State the required arrival date and distinguish production completion from transit time. Confirm the delivery method, trade terms and schedule in the quotation.
