BUYER QUESTIONS

Clear answers before the next step.

Can I enquire before choosing a model?

Yes. Describe the intended use, quantity and arrival date; add a reference or drawing if available.

What determines MOQ and lead time?

The model, materials, design versions, decoration, packaging and tooling affect the proposal. Small batches from 10 pieces and fast sampling apply to eligible projects; confirm the conditions in your quote.

What should my enquiry include?

A model or product idea, quantity, artwork, pack contents, destination and required arrival date are a useful start.

What matters for this project

For collapsible models, define target capacity and both folded and open dimensions.

  • Drink or recipient and the intended service setting
  • Quantity per design and exact contents of a set
  • Required arrival date and delivery destination

Turn the idea into a reviewable brief

Start with a model reference. Then align artwork, sample approval and packing with the project’s deadline.

  • Identify artwork versions and approval owner
  • We will help you review the sample against actual use
  • Separate personal gifts from shared service items

Choose the Packed Format

Compare the folded and expanded dimensions of a collapsible cup, or select a silicone or compact plastic model for the drinks you plan to serve. Include the lid and packing requirements in your shortlist.

Customize Your Camp Drinkware

We prepare a free design proposal and visual mockup on the selected cup. Silicone cups use color-changing ink; plastic cups use screen printing. Send the model, quantity and artwork to niles@boomdrinkware.com.

Questions Before You Order

Which camping cup formats can I compare?

The current selection includes collapsible cups, silicone drinkware, compact plastic cup formats and bottle-cap openers. Open a listed model to compare its material, capacity, dimensions and style choices.

Can you design cups for our camping group?

Yes. Send the selected model or cup idea, quantity and your logo or artwork. We provide a free design proposal and product visual mockup for your camping group.
