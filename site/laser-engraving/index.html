BUYING GUIDE

Check Laser Decoration Compatibility

Laser decoration depends on the exact substrate, surface and coating. Discuss the chosen model first, especially when evaluating plastic or silicone drinkware rather than metal cups.

Points to confirm for laser marked cups
Review areaWhat to confirm
Model and surfaceIdentify the actual cup, material and surface finish before selecting the method.
Artwork and sampleReview placement, colour or pattern requirements on a physical reference.
Approval criteriaAgree appearance limits and care instructions for the decorated product.

Can every plastic or silicone cup be laser marked?

No universal compatibility should be assumed. Ask for a material-specific feasibility review and sample. Compare screen printing or other decoration methods when laser treatment is unsuitable for the chosen surface.
