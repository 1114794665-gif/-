Quality Starts Before Production

The best time to solve a quality problem is before it reaches mass production. We identify potential risks in materials, leakage, heat resistance, coating, colour, branding and packaging early in the process.

Prevent problems early. Protect production quality.

Pre-Production Quality Planning

Materials and Sample Approval

Clear standards before production help prevent costly mistakes during mass production.

  • Material and component inspection
  • Approved sample confirmation
  • Product specification review
  • Pantone colour and artwork approval
  • Packaging and labelling confirmation
  • Testing requirements confirmation

During Manufacturing

In-Process Quality Control

Our QC team checks key production steps to catch problems early. We inspect:

  • Product size and structure
  • Rim and stem strength
  • Surface finish and colour
  • Logo quality and position
  • Plastic-to-silicone part fit

After Manufacturing

Final Inspection Before Shipment

Finished products are checked against the approved sample and order requirements. We inspect:

  • Seal and leakage
  • Appearance and surface finish
  • Logo quality and position
  • Quantity and accessories
  • Barcodes, packaging and cartons

Testing Room

Product testing before shipment

Our testing room verifies product performance and quality before mass delivery. Key tests include colour consistency, heat resistance, print adhesion, silicone rebound, leak and seal, and transport packaging, so products meet the approved specification and your market requirements.
