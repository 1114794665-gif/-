Manufacturing Experience Since 2013

BOOM Drinkware is the international drinkware division of Shenzhen Baoyuanfeng Precision Mould Co., Ltd., a custom drinkware manufacturer specialising in silicone and plastic cups, mugs, tumblers and bar tools.

We help brands and distributors turn product ideas into scalable, market-ready drinkware with greater efficiency and fewer sourcing risks — because tooling, moulding, printing and packing all happen under one roof.

Our ODM services enable customers to customise existing models with their preferred colours, finishes, logos and packaging. Our OEM capabilities support complete product development from drawing to mass production.

OEM Service

Turn your product idea into a manufacturable, market-ready product — drawings, tooling, samples and mass production in one line.

ODM Service

Choose an existing model and customise the colour, logo, finish, lid and packaging to your brief.

More Than a Manufacturer

We provide complete drinkware solutions to help your brand develop, produce and deliver the right products.

Welcome to our factory

After years of working with global drinkware buyers, we kept hearing the same problem: buyers could find many factories, but still faced slow communication, inconsistent quality, long product development and little support between sampling and mass production. BOOM Drinkware was created to bridge reliable manufacturing with professional international service. Our goal is to make every project clearer, more efficient and easier to manage — from the first idea to repeat production.
