WHAT YOU CAN CUSTOMIZE

Five specification axes across the series

Each axis is confirmed per style and per order

Finish: plain, red, translucent or full color

The recorded styles cover plain cups, red party cups, translucent cups and full color printing. Pick the finish first, then the capacity.

Capacity: 2 oz to 24 oz

2 oz, 10 oz, 12 oz, 16 oz, 17 oz, 20 oz, 22 oz and 24 oz are recorded. Any other capacity is reviewed as a custom mold project.

Lid and straw

Two styles already ship as a lid and straw version (12 oz and 20 oz). On the other styles a lid and straw can be added as a configured option, subject to rim diameter.

Material: PP or PS

PP covers the reusable stadium cups. The 2 oz party cups are recorded in PS. Confirm the material with the quote if you need one material across the whole order.

Custom print and logo

Full color print is recorded on three styles; the rest are quoted for screen print or UV hard print. Usable print area is confirmed per model.

Buying Questions

Which specifications are recorded for this model?

Model TYCB-001 records PP as the material. Recorded capacities across the stadium cup series run from 2 oz to 24 oz.

How is logo customization reviewed?

Send your logo, colour references and preferred placement. The suitable method and usable area must be reviewed on this model before approving artwork or a sample.

What should I include in an enquiry?

Include model TYCB-001, estimated quantity, intended use, delivery country, required arrival date and artwork. Sample arrangements, minimum order and lead time are confirmed for the project.
