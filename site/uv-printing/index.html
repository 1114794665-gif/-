FAQ

UV Printing questions, answered

Rotary UV printing is a digital decoration method for curved drinkware. Ink is cured instantly by UV light after printing. It supports 1-color and 2-color spot logos as well as full-color CMYK graphics. White underbase and clear protective varnish are optional.

Can UV printing be applied to every cup material?

Compatibility depends on the surface, coating, shape and ink system. Ask for a decorated sample of the selected model. Confirm adhesion, care instructions and any claimed durability against the agreed test criteria.
