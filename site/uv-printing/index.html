Questions Before You Order

What are your payment terms?

We require a 30% deposit. The remaining 70% must be paid in full before shipment.

Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept T/T (bank transfer), Western Union and Alipay.

Which currencies can I pay in?

We accept USD, CNY, EUR and GBP. Other currencies can also be arranged for your order.

Which trade terms do you offer?

We offer EXW (Ex Works), FOB Shenzhen, C&F and DDP. The trade term is agreed with you for each order.

How does the cup shape affect my UV artwork layout?

The layout follows the selected silicone cup shape and usable decoration area. Send the cup reference and artwork; our free mockup shows the position and scale on that model.

Can you develop UV artwork from a reference image rather than a finished logo file?

Yes. Send your image, sketch or written idea. We develop a free design proposal and show the artwork on the selected silicone cup in a product mockup.

Do the Tritan wash-test figures apply to silicone cups?

For the tested Tritan printing configuration, home dishwasher durability is 200–300 cycles without colour loss; the special ink upgrade reaches up to 500 cycles. Commercial high-temperature, strong-alkali washing lasts about 100–150 cycles. These results do not apply to silicone cups.
