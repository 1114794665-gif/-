BUYER QUESTIONS

Clear answers before the next step.

Does the resin name prove food-contact or cleaning suitability?

No. We will help you review the finished model, components, decoration, intended conditions and applicable records for your order.

Can I enquire before choosing a model?

Yes. Describe the intended use, quantity and arrival date; add a reference or drawing if available.

Give Your Next Sourcing Decision a Clearer Direction

Bring these decisions back to your actual product, drink or brand project. Clear specifications, samples and approval targets help your team work from the same brief.

Can one drinkware material be assumed suitable for every market and use?

Yes. Compare the intended drink, care routine and decoration requirements with model-specific material information. Ask for the evidence relevant to the product and destination rather than assuming one material meets every market or application.

What should the quotation and sample review include?

For drinkware material guide: match use, print & care, provide the use, model or reference, intended quantity and the information needed to review drink conditions, decoration and relevant evidence. Identify essential requirements and undecided items so the quote and sample review address the same brief.

How should a fixed project date be handled?

State the required arrival date and destination at the start. We will assist you with artwork or design, sample approval, production and transit milestones separately. We will help you review the timing impact of any change to an approved specification; service scope and timing need project confirmation.
