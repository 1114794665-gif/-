BUYER QUESTIONS

Clear answers before the next step.

Does the resin name prove food-contact or cleaning suitability?

No. We will help you review the finished model, components, decoration, intended conditions and applicable records for your order.

Can I enquire before choosing a model?

Yes. Describe the intended use, quantity and arrival date; add a reference or drawing if available.

A material name is not a complete specification

Capacity, wall geometry, components, finish and decoration also affect a product decision. Do not transfer one model’s heat, cleaning or test claim to every product using that material name.

Connect material selection to decoration

Our confirmed routes include screen printing on plastic cups, shot cups and ice buckets; UV printing and color-changing ink on silicone cups; and heat transfer decoration on ice buckets. Keep the selected model and component in the proposal.
