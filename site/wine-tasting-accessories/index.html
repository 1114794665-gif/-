THREE ACCESSORIES, THREE JOBS

What a tasting table actually needs

The spittoon: what happens after the sip

A tasting pour is sipped, judged and discarded. Without a dedicated vessel, that means repeated trips or a shared bucket at the end of the table. A spittoon keeps the tasting station self-contained, which matters when the table is in a retail aisle, a cellar door or a hired venue.

  • PP body, wipe-clean inside and out
  • Wide opening for a discreet, aimed pour
  • Logo space on the front panel

The glass and the flight taster: what the wine is judged in

A tasting glass narrows the nose and standardises the pour; a flight taster lets one guest work through three samples without three separate glasses. Both are reusable, which is the point for a venue running tastings every week.

  • ISO-style tasting glass, 220 ml / 7.5 oz, Tritan
  • Silicone flight taster, 6 oz, flexible and packable
  • Both accept a printed or UV-cured logo

MATERIAL DECISIONS

Tritan, PP or silicone — what changes on the table

Tritan for the tasting glass

Clear, rigid and glass-like in the hand, without the breakage of real glass in a venue. The ISO-style bowl keeps the pour and the nose consistent between guests, which is the reason tasting rooms standardise on this shape.

PP for the spittoon

Opaque, tough and easy to wipe down between sessions. PP holds its shape when the vessel is lifted and emptied repeatedly, which is what a spittoon actually has to survive.

Silicone for the flight taster

Flexible walls survive being packed into a case, stacked in a tray or dropped on a terrace. For a flight served away from a table — a vineyard walk, a pop-up, a tasting counter with no glass storage — silicone is the version that travels.

BUYER QUESTIONS

Clear answers before the next step.

What capacity is the spittoon?

We do not publish a litre figure for BT-016 until the factory confirms it against the mould drawing for your order. Ask for the confirmed capacity in your quotation and it will be stated there.

Is the tasting glass real glass?

No. HJB-073 is Tritan, 220 ml / 7.5 oz, in the ISO-style shape used for consistent tasting pours. It is reusable and does not shatter like glass in a venue.

Can all three carry our logo?

Yes — screen or UV printing on the PP spittoon, the Tritan glass and the silicone taster. Print area, colour count and artwork version are confirmed on the proof.

Can the three items ship together?

Yes, when they are ordered on one purchase order. Packing stays separated per item so cartons can be distributed to different venues.

What is the minimum order?

Eligible stock models start from 10 pieces; new tooling starts from 10,000 pieces. The figure for your specific combination is confirmed in the quotation.

Do you supply tasting trays or flight boards?

Not as a standard model. A flight board would be a new mould; the silicone flight taster is the current way to serve three pours from one set.
