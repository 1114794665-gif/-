FAQ

Heat Transfer Printing questions, answered

The graphic is printed on a PET carrier before application, then transferred with heat and pressure. UV printing deposits and cures ink directly on the object. The suitable method depends on the actual material and geometry.

Questions Before You Order

What are your payment terms?

We require a 30% deposit. The remaining 70% must be paid in full before shipment.

Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept T/T (bank transfer), Western Union and Alipay.

Which currencies can I pay in?

We accept USD, CNY, EUR and GBP. Other currencies can also be arranged for your order.

Which trade terms do you offer?

We offer EXW (Ex Works), FOB Shenzhen, C&F and DDP. The trade term is agreed with you for each order.

Should I send the ice bucket shape before the artwork is finalized?

Yes. Select the bucket reference first. We develop the heat transfer layout around that bucket body and provide a free visual mockup of the proposed artwork.

Can I choose screen printing instead of heat transfer for an ice bucket?

Yes. We offer screen printing and heat transfer decoration for ice buckets. Send your selected bucket and artwork so our factory team can prepare the decoration proposal for that project.
