Plastic Cup Color Matching: Body Color and Printed Logos

Treat the cup body, printed ink and product image as separate color references. We will help you finalise the actual material and artwork combination rather than relying on a screen image alone.

Updated: 2026-11-24 · BOOM Drinkware

Why the reference needs a material

A reference color viewed on paper or a display is not the finished cup. The resin, finish, thickness and viewing conditions affect how the product appears. Give the intended color reference together with the selected model and material.

Separate the body and the logo

Record the cup-body reference and each logo color independently. A color mockup shows the design direction and placement; a physical sample shows the actual product combination. Do not treat two different cup finishes as equivalent color approvals.

Write the production reference

Identify the approved sample, artwork version and color references in the order. Any numerical tolerance needs its own measurement method, conditions and agreed acceptance criterion. This guide does not invent a universal BOOM color-difference tolerance.
