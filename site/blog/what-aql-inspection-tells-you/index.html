What AQL Inspection Does—and Does Not—Tell You

Acceptance sampling uses a sample and an agreed decision rule to assess a production lot. Passing a sampling plan is not proof that every item in that lot is defect-free.

Updated: 2026-11-24 · BOOM Drinkware

Define the plan before the result

Record the lot, sampling method, sample size, defect categories and acceptance rule. A percentage printed beside the letters AQL is not a complete inspection plan. Product dimensions, print appearance, assembly and packing can require different checks and acceptance criteria.

Separate a sample result from a population claim

An inspection observes the sampled items and applies the stated rule. It does not inspect every item unless the work scope explicitly requires that. Do not interpret a plan label as a measured percentage of defective goods in the entire shipment.

Use the approved product reference

The selected model, approved artwork and sample define what is checked. Keep the inspection record tied to the actual lot and configuration. Use the NIST acceptance-sampling overview for the statistical concept; a particular order’s inspection plan belongs to its quality agreement.
