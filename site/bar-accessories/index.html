Choose accessories by the serving task

Start with the job your accessory needs to do: open the bottle, control the pour or close the bottle between servings. Compare the listed models by material, bottle-neck fit and gift format. Follow each product link for its dimensions, packaging and order minimum.

Print a logo or engrave a suitable manual tool

We place your artwork on suitable plastic accessories and offer laser engraving on compatible manual bar tools. Send the accessory model and artwork for a free visual mockup showing the logo position on the selected item.

Build a bulk accessory order

Email niles@boomdrinkware.com with the accessory references, quantity, logo colors, packaging, destination and required arrival date. For printed logos, the color minimums are 250 pieces for one color, 1,000 for two colors and 2,000 for three colors. The product model also has its own minimum; both requirements apply.

Questions Before You Order

Which bar accessories can I customize with my logo?

Choose from our listed wine stoppers, pourers and bottle openers. Send the model references and your logo or idea. We provide free design proposals and product mockups showing the artwork on each selected accessory.

Can you personalize manual bar tools with laser engraving?

Yes. We offer laser engraving on suitable manual bar tools. Send the selected model and artwork so the mockup shows the engraving position on the chosen accessory.

How do I choose an accessory for a specific bottle?

Compare the listed accessory model with your bottle-neck dimensions and intended serving task. Send the bottle reference together with the stopper, pourer or opener model so we can match the project brief to the correct product.

What information do you need for a wholesale accessory quote?

Email niles@boomdrinkware.com with the accessory models, quantities, logo colors, packaging, destination and required arrival date. We quote the selected products and decoration as one project.

What is the minimum quantity for printed bar accessories?

One-color logo printing starts at 250 pieces, two colors at 1,000 and three colors at 2,000. Each product model also has its own minimum order quantity; both requirements apply.
