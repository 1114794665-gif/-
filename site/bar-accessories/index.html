Can coolers, koozies and bottle sleeves

Can coolers and koozies are made in silicone and neoprene styles, including slim can sizes, zip-up bottle sleeves and personalised runs for weddings, breweries and promotions. Fit is the deciding factor: send the can or bottle dimensions, or a physical sample, and we match the sleeve to it. Logos are applied by printing for silicone and neoprene; bulk and personalised orders run on the same line.

Coasters, trays, ice buckets and serving tools

Bar accessories extend to coasters, serving trays, ice buckets, stir sticks and straws in plastic and silicone. Sizes are produced to your specification rather than taken from a fixed list, and the logo method follows the material — printing for plastic and silicone, laser marking for metal components.

Do you make slim can koozies?

Slim can sizes are produced alongside standard can coolers. Send the can dimensions or a physical sample so the sleeve is matched to the actual container.

Can I order personalised koozies in bulk?

Personalised and bulk koozie orders run on the same line in silicone and neoprene. Send the quantity, artwork and container dimensions.

Are neoprene and zip-up bottle koozies available?

Neoprene styles and zip-up bottle sleeves are available. Construction and closure are confirmed against your bottle before production.
