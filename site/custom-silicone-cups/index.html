PROCESS FIT / BY BODY TYPE

Which decoration fits which cup

Ten body types, seven processes. Solid means we recommend it for that shape — best cost, best yield. Outline means it can be done, with the caveat in the last column. Anything not listed does not hold up in use, so we will not quote it.

RecommendedPossible, with a caveat
Body type
Decoration that fits

What decides it

Beer & pint cupsPP · PS · PC · Tritan
Screen printHeat transferColour-change inkUV printHydro dip

The straight taper takes a screen cleanly. PP goes through a flame pass first — skip it and the ink lifts with a thumb.

Handled mugs & steinsPC · PP
Screen printHydro dipUV printColour-change ink

The handle blocks the screen, so print lands either side of it. For a full wraparound, hydro dip is the only route.

Stemmed wine, champagne & cocktailAcrylic · PS · PET · Tritan
Screen printLaser engravingUV printHeat transferColour-change ink

A laser on acrylic leaves a frosty white mark — the cleanest logo these shapes take. Print goes on the bowl, never the stem.

Shot & shooter glassesPS · Acrylic · PP
Screen printUV printLaser engravingColour-change ink

Short body: there is no room for a full wrap. One colour or a small mark works better than a big graphic.

Tumblers & double-wall straw cupsAcrylic · PP · Tritan · PC
Heat transferUV printScreen printLaser engraving

A full wrap is heat transfer work. On a double wall only the outside can be printed — never between the walls.

Shaped cups (bat, fishbowl, sphere)PP · PS · Acrylic
Hydro dipUV printColour-change ink

The curvature is too tight for a screen or a transfer. Only hydro dipping wraps the whole body.

Colour-change cups (cold or heat)PP · PS
Colour-change inkScreen print (base)UV print

A white base goes down first, then the thermochromic layer, and both are cured. A plain moulded body cannot change colour — it needs printed area.

Silicone cups (pint, stein, barrel)Silicone · compression moulded
Colour in the materialMoulded-in 3D logoSilicone screen / pad print

Silicone takes its colour in the material, not on the surface. Printing needs a silicone-specific ink and a second heat cure.

Collapsible silicone cupsSilicone + nylon ring
Colour in the materialSilicone pad print (away from folds)

Ink on a fold cracks after a couple of collapses. Print only on the flat band of the body.

Silicone tumblers & shot cupsSilicone, with lid and straw
Colour in the materialMoulded-in 3D logoSilicone screen print

Lids and straws can be moulded in a second colour to make a set. Small parts take no large print — keep the mark small and simple.

Every body type on this page is our own mould, moulded and decorated in our own plant — so the entry is 10 pcs, plain or printed, one colour or many. Screens and ink matching are charged as setup on top of the unit price, never as a minimum order. See the four order tiers →
