Questions Before You Order

Can I customize silicone cups with lids?

Yes. Choose a listed cup with the required lid configuration and send the model reference, artwork and quantity. We prepare a free design proposal and product mockup for that cup.

Which printing services do you offer for silicone cups?

We offer UV printing and color-changing ink for silicone cups. Send your logo or pattern and the chosen model for a free design proposal showing the artwork on that product.

What should I compare when choosing a collapsible silicone cup?

Compare the open capacity, folded dimensions and lid configuration shown for each listed model. Send the cup reference, logo, quantity and intended use so the quotation covers the required cup and accessories.

Do you supply custom silicone shot glasses?

Yes. Our listed silicone shot-cup range includes 1.5 oz and 2 oz styles. Compare those models on our custom shot glasses page and send the model and artwork for a free visual mockup.

What do you need for a wholesale silicone cup quote?

Send the model or shape idea, quantity, artwork, lid requirements, packaging and destination to niles@boomdrinkware.com. We quote the selected cup and decoration together.
