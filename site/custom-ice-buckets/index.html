Choose the bucket before the branding

Start with the service setup and the bottle you plan to use. Capacity alone does not confirm bottle fit or how much room remains for ice.

Size & bottle fit
Send bottle dimensions, the intended bottle count and any space limit. Confirm fit on the selected model rather than assuming a bottle count from litres.
LED or non-LED
Compare the listed model groups. For an LED bucket, confirm the light effect, power arrangement and care instructions for that model.
Logo & appearance
Share artwork, logo placement and a colour reference. We will help you review the usable print area and decoration method for the chosen material.
Packing & delivery
State the order quantity, pack contents, destination and required arrival date. MOQ and timing are confirmed in your quotation.

Before you request a quote

Can I order custom printed ice buckets without LED lighting?

Use the non-LED model group to shortlist a bucket, then share the quantity and artwork. Decoration options are reviewed for the selected model and material.

What should I send for an ice bucket quotation?

Include your model shortlist or intended use, bottle dimensions, quantity, logo artwork, packaging requirements, destination and arrival date. If you need help choosing, describe the service setup.
