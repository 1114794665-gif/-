BUYING GUIDE

Plan Branded LED Ice Buckets

Browse BOOM Drinkware custom led ice buckets by model, material and capacity. Review product references and discuss logo options, quantities and delivery requirements.

Points to confirm for custom ice buckets
Review areaWhat to confirm
Bottle fitProvide bottle dimensions, the intended number of bottles and the available service space.
Logo visibilityReview artwork position and contrast under the intended venue lighting.
Power and careConfirm the light module, charging or battery arrangement and cleaning instructions.

How do I choose an LED ice bucket for bottle service?

Start with your bottle sizes and venue use. Confirm the bucket dimensions, intended loading, light effect and logo placement on a specific model. Ask for the applicable care, electrical and shipping information before ordering.
