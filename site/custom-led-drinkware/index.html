FROM BRIEF TO PACKED PRODUCT

The LED production workflow.

A planning sequence for custom LED drinkware and bar accessories. The actual routing, tooling and checks are agreed for the selected design.

01Brief02Shell03LED module04Branding05Assembly06Inspection07Packing
01Define the product+

Start with a cup, ice bucket or bar accessory. Agree the intended use, dimensions, logo, quantity, destination and required arrival date. New shapes need a separate tooling review.

Approval point
Approve the product brief and sample requirements.

02Form the shell+

Review the specified resin, tool and shell geometry. Prepare the cup or housing and check the light-transmitting area, module fit and part interfaces for the selected design.

Approval point
Check part appearance, dimensions and fit.

03Configure the LED module+

Select a compatible light module and define colours, modes and activation. Review battery or charging arrangements and access to the power compartment. Any circuit changes require a separate technical review.

Approval point
Approve the light effect and power configuration on a sample.

04Apply the branding+

Choose an appropriate logo process for the substrate and artwork. Review location, coverage and contrast with the light both on and off; keep controls and light paths clear.

Approval point
Approve the physical decoration sample.

05Assemble the product+

Fit the light module, power components and housing in the agreed sequence. Check the mounting, closures and any lids or accessories against the approved sample.

Approval point
Check assembly, switches and component retention.

06Inspect against the sample+

Check the specified lighting modes, activation, appearance, decoration and fit. Agree runtime, ingress or destination-specific testing separately where the project requires it.

Approval point
Use the agreed inspection and acceptance plan.

07Pack for the order+

Confirm individual packaging, protective separators, cartons, care information and battery details. Review applicable transport documents for the actual battery and destination before dispatch.

Approval point
Approve packaging, instructions and shipping scope.

Illustrations explain the workflow; they do not represent factory photographs or certified test results.

BUYER QUESTIONS

LED customization, explained.

What can I customize on LED drinkware?

Start with the product model, logo, body colour and packaging. Lighting colours, modes, activation and power options depend on the selected module and housing. New tooling or electronics changes are reviewed separately.

Do all LED cups have the same light effects or batteries?

No. Review the actual model and sample. Button, pressure or liquid activation, colour-changing effects, battery access and charging arrangements are model-specific.

Can I add a logo to light-up cups, ice buckets and coasters?

Share the model and vector artwork. Confirm substrate compatibility, print area and readability with the light switched on and off before approving the decoration sample.

What are the MOQ and production lead time?

They depend on the model, artwork, lighting configuration, packaging and development scope. Send estimated quantities and the required arrival date for a project quotation. Stock purchase minimums are not custom-logo minimums.

Are LED drinkware products waterproof or dishwasher safe?

Do not assume either property. Follow the care instructions for the selected model, keep electrical components away from moisture as instructed, and request relevant test information for any special requirement.

Is glow-in-the-dark or UV-reactive drinkware the same as LED drinkware?

No. LED products use an electrical light source. UV-reactive and glow-in-the-dark materials work differently; they are not included in this LED model selection unless the product also has an LED module.

What should I send for an LED product quotation?

Send model numbers or reference images, estimated quantities, intended use, logo artwork, desired lighting, power preference, packaging and destination. Identify any required tests or documents so the quotation can define their scope.
