How should the body, accessories and gift packing be specified?

Use an itemised specification for the opener, stand, cutter, stopper and power components as applicable. State each relevant material separately; a body-material record does not define the entire kit.

What should I review on a sample before a bulk order?

Agree the review points and acceptance criteria for the selected model, then check the sample, artwork and packing. These are review areas, not claims that a model has passed a performance test.

Opening functionCheck the approved opening action with the intended cap or cork and model instructions.
Body and moving partsReview the finish, edges, assembly and applicable moving components on a sample.
Powered configurationSpecify PD power supply for electric openers and agree the included power components, connector and any battery information for the selected kit.
Branding and presentationApprove the marking area, artwork, accessory layout and gift or bulk packing.

What should I send for a complete and comparable quote?

Send the models, quantity per model, intended use, artwork, accessories, packing, destination and required arrival. Ask the quote to identify product, decoration, sample and delivery charges separately.

  1. Choose by cap or cork; specify aluminum and ABS components with PD power for electric models.
  2. List the artwork, colours and components rather than inferring kit contents from an image.
  3. Agree the sample, acceptance criteria, quote scope and delivery terms.

Buying Questions

Which opener should I choose for a branded promotion?

Choose by the container closure first. Use a crown-cap opener for compatible capped bottles, or a wine corkscrew for the intended cork. Then compare body shape, material, logo area and gift packaging.

Does an electric opener kit include a base and accessories?

Inclusions vary by model. Request a component list for the selected kit; a reference image or a component material record is not proof that every pictured attachment is supplied.

What materials and power supply do the electric wine openers use?

Our electric wine openers use aluminum and ABS components and PD power supply. Confirm the selected kit contents and included power components in the quotation. Connector, voltage, wattage and battery details are not specified by the PD label alone.

What is the minimum order quantity for custom cups?

The minimum order quantity is 10 pieces. Include the model, artwork, colour, packing and required quantity when requesting a project quotation.

How much do custom cups with a logo cost?

A project quote is needed because the model, quantity, decoration, accessories, packing and delivery affect the price. Compare the same specification and ask which setup, sample, tooling and delivery charges are included. External reference prices are not published here as confirmed selling prices.

Can I add my logo to a cup, coaster or opener?

Request a decoration review for the selected model, surface and logo position. The artwork and finished appearance should be approved before bulk production. Provide the design, intended size and colour references. Do not assume that a marking method suitable for one material will work on every product.

Can I request samples before a bulk order?

Yes. Discuss an existing model sample, a decorated sample or a pre-production sample for the selected project. Confirm the sample specification, charges, preparation time, freight and any credit terms in writing. A digital proof checks layout and does not replace physical sample approval.
