Buying Questions

Which opener should I choose for a branded promotion?

Choose by the container closure first. Use a crown-cap opener for compatible capped bottles, or a wine corkscrew for the intended cork. Then compare body shape, material, logo area and gift packaging.

What is the minimum order quantity for custom cups?

The minimum order quantity is 10 pieces. Include the model, artwork, colour, packing and required quantity when requesting a project quotation.

How much do custom cups with a logo cost?

A project quote is needed because the model, quantity, decoration, accessories, packing and delivery affect the price. Compare the same specification and ask which setup, sample, tooling and delivery charges are included. External reference prices are not published here as confirmed selling prices.

Can I add my logo to a cup, coaster or opener?

Request a decoration review for the selected model, surface and logo position. The artwork and finished appearance should be approved before bulk production. Provide the design, intended size and colour references. Do not assume that a marking method suitable for one material will work on every product.

Can I request samples before a bulk order?

Yes. Discuss an existing model sample, a decorated sample or a pre-production sample for the selected project. We will help you confirm the sample specification, charges, preparation time, freight and any credit terms in writing. A digital proof checks layout and does not replace physical sample approval.

Questions Before You Order

What is the difference between a bottle cap opener and a wine corkscrew?

A cap opener removes crown caps from beer and soda bottles. A corkscrew removes a wine cork. Choose the opening function first, then select the material, dimensions and format of the listed model.

Can you print or laser engrave my logo on an opener?

Yes. We offer logo printing on bottle openers and laser engraving for manual bar tools. Our free design proposal and product mockup show the artwork on the selected component. Manual bar tools come first, and LED or powered drinkware can also be laser marked; silicone is not part of the laser service.

What opener materials are shown here?

Examples include the KPQ-005 ABS beer opener, KPQ-025 stainless-steel and zinc-alloy credit-card opener, and JS-039 zinc-alloy wing corkscrew. Use the selected model page for its complete specifications.

Do logo-color minimums also apply to engraving?

The color thresholds are for printed logos: 250 pieces for one color, 1,000 for two and 2,000 for three. Engraving is a separate process; its order quantity is quoted for the selected model and engraving project.

Can you create the artwork from an idea?

Yes. Send your logo, sketch, reference or written idea. We provide a free design proposal and visual mockup showing the design on the selected opener.

How do I request a bulk opener quote?

Email niles@boomdrinkware.com with the model or opening function, quantity, artwork, printing or engraving requirement, packaging and destination. Add your required arrival date to the project brief.
