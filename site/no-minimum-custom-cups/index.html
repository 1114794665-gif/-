BUYER QUESTIONS

Clear answers before the next step.

Can I enquire before choosing a model?

Yes. Describe the intended use, quantity and arrival date; add a reference or drawing if available.

What determines MOQ and lead time?

The model, materials, design versions, decoration, packaging and tooling affect the proposal. Small batches from 10 pieces and fast sampling apply to eligible projects; confirm the conditions in your quote.

What should my enquiry include?

A model or product idea, quantity, artwork, pack contents, destination and required arrival date are a useful start.
