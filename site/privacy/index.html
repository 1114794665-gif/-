Enquiry Information & File Handling

Understand how contact details, project requirements and uploaded artwork are used for enquiry follow-up, and how to contact us about a submission.

Prepare a Project Enquiry →

What you submit

An enquiry includes the contact details and project requirements you enter, plus the files you choose to upload. Send only material you are authorized to share. Do not upload passwords, payment information or unrelated personal data.

Receipt & purpose

The website receiving service stores submissions for model selection, quotation and enquiry follow-up. Keep the enquiry reference returned after submission. Storage and email notification are separate states; email forwarding is not configured in this preview.

Questions & removal requests

Contact [email protected] with the enquiry reference to ask about a submission or request file removal. Requests require review and confirmation; this notice does not promise automatic deletion or an unconfirmed response deadline.

Browser functions & measurement

Necessary functions such as model comparison may retain state in the browser. No GA4 measurement ID is currently configured, and enquiry content is not sent to GA4. If analytics is enabled, its purpose and choices should be explained. Names, emails, phone numbers, enquiry text and artwork must not be included in website analytics.

Before public launch

The operator needs to confirm access roles, hosting processors, retention and backup handling. If these arrangements affect your sharing decision, discuss them through the project email before sending sensitive business files.

Make the Next Step Specific

Share the model, use, estimated quantity, artwork, destination and required arrival date. Identify any items you have not decided.
