Packaging: Protection First, Presentation Second

A cup that breaks in transit is a product problem, whatever the box looked like. Packing is planned together with the specification — the box, the insert, the carton and the label — because each one is decided by where the product is sold.

THE PACKAGING LAYERS

Three layers, three different jobs.

Most packaging problems come from treating these as one decision. The inner pack protects the surface, the retail unit sells the product, and the master carton survives the journey.

Inner pack — protection

Poly bag, tissue wrap, foam or bubble pouch, or a moulded pulp cradle. This is the layer that decides whether the print arrives scuffed, and it is the cheapest place to add protection.

Bulk and retail

Retail unit — presentation

White box, kraft box, printed carton, sleeve, window box or a gift set with an insert. The dieline, the print method and the board grade all change the price per unit.

Printed per artwork

Master carton — transit

Carton grade, pack count, weight limit, shipping marks, barcode and pallet pattern. This is the layer your forwarder and your warehouse will judge you on.

BEFORE THE BOX IS MADE

What decides whether the box works.

A beautiful dieline on the wrong board arrives as a crushed carton. These are the checks we run before a packaging order is placed.

01

Dieline and product fit

Internal dimensions against the actual product, with clearance for the bag or wrap. A 2 mm error is a rattling product or a box that will not close.

02

Board grade and print method

Corrugated flute, greyboard, kraft or art paper — and whether the artwork is printed, laminated or applied as a label. Each has a different cost floor.

03

Insert and protection

Whether the product needs a cradle, a divider or a bag, and how it behaves in a drop test — a stemmed glass and a tumbler need completely different answers.

04

Labels and barcodes

Barcode symbology, position, and whether the retailer requires a specific label size. Barcodes are scan-tested, not just printed.

05

Master carton rules

Pack count, carton weight limit, inner dimensions, shipping marks and pallet pattern — set by the channel, not by us.

06

Channel requirements

Amazon FBA, a retail chain and a distributor each have their own carton, labelling and appointment rules. Send the requirement sheet if you have one.

Can you design the packaging?

We can propose a structure and a dieline from your product and artwork. If you have a designer, send the file and we will check it against the product and the board grade before sampling.

Do you have a minimum for printed boxes?

Yes, and it is separate from the cup minimum. Tell us the box type and the print coverage and it is quoted as its own line — a printed box run and a moulded cup run do not share a setup.

Can you pack to Amazon FBA rules?

Yes. Send the FBA shipment plan: carton weight and size limits, FNSKU labelling, poly-bag suffocation warnings and the appointment process. The packing is built to that sheet, not to a generic standard.

What if the cartons arrive damaged?

Carton damage is a transit issue, and it is handled with photos, the packing list and the forwarder's claim process. Drop-testing the packed carton before shipment is what prevents most of it.

Can I supply my own packaging?

Yes. Send it to our address and we will pack against it, with the arrival date planned before production starts. Supplying packaging late is the most common reason a shipment slips.
