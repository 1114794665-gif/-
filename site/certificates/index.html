05 / PRODUCTION PROCESS

Nine steps from a drawing to a packed carton

This is the sequence an order actually follows. Which moulding step applies depends on the product: injection for rigid parts and stems, blow moulding for thin-wall cups, compression moulding for silicone. No temperature, cycle time or inspection threshold is published here — those are agreed per project.

01

Design and 3D review

Work from your artwork or a reference sample. A 3D file is issued and signed off before anything is cut.

02

Tooling and trial

CNC-cut mould, then trial shots. Dimensions and appearance are checked on the trial part, not on a promise.

03

Material and colour

Resin grade or silicone compound is fixed, and colour is matched to an approved chip or Pantone reference.

04

Moulding

Injection for rigid parts, blow moulding for thin-wall cups, compression moulding for silicone. Process parameters are locked per mould.

05

Trimming and deflashing

Gates and flash are removed and the rim and joint faces are dressed so parts assemble without force.

06

Decoration

Screen print, pad print, heat transfer, water transfer, laser or colour-change ink — chosen per design, not per convenience.

07

Post-cure

Silicone parts get a second high-temperature cure to stabilise the compound and drive off residual odour.

08

Inspection

Appearance, key dimensions, seal and assembly fit are checked against the approved sample before packing.

09

Packing and dispatch

Cartons are labelled to your specification and photographed before the container is sealed.

BUYER QUESTIONS

Before you quote one of these to your buyer

Can I see the full report, not just page one?

Yes. Tell us the model and the market you are selling into, and we send the PDF that actually covers it. What we will not do is send a report for a different grade and call it close enough.

Whose certificates are these?

The management system certificates and the patents are held by Hongrun New Material Technology, our affiliated production plant. The material reports are issued to the resin suppliers. Every file is labelled by holder on this page, so nothing is implied.

Does a patent give me the right to sell the design?

No. The patent protects the structure. Your right to sell a particular design comes from the agreement we sign. Tell us the market and we will say what applies before you commit to a quantity.

Will you test my order?

Third-party testing can be arranged against the standard your market requires. Scope, sample and cost are agreed before it starts, and the report is issued to you or to us as you prefer.

Ask for the file that matches your model

Send the model number, the market you are selling into and the document your buyer asked for. We will tell you which of these files applies, and which test still has to be run.
