Questions Before You Order

Can I order light up cups with lids and straws?

Yes. Browse our listed tumbler and to-go styles for lid and straw configurations. Send the model reference, required quantity and logo for a free design proposal and a quotation for that cup configuration.

Can you print my logo on light up cups?

Yes. We place the artwork on the printable cup body and prepare a free product mockup showing the logo on your selected model. Lighting modules and controls are separate from the printable surface.

How do light up cups work?

The cup uses an LED lighting assembly. Activation, power configuration and light modes depend on the selected model. Include your preferred lighting appearance in the brief; the quotation identifies the model and its configuration.

Can I order light up cups in bulk for an event?

Yes. Send the cup model, quantity, logo colors, packaging, destination and required arrival date. We provide free design and a product mockup. The product minimum and applicable printed-logo minimum are both included in the order brief.

Are LED light up cups the same as glow-in-the-dark cups?

No. LED cups use an electronic lighting assembly. Glow-in-the-dark cups use a different material or finish effect. Choose the product reference for the effect you want and send it with your event brief.
