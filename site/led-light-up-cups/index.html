BUYING GUIDE

Specify LED Cups Before Ordering

Browse EVERCUP led light up cups by model, material and capacity. Review product references and discuss logo options, quantities and delivery requirements.

Points to confirm for LED light up cups
Review areaWhat to confirm
Light effectConfirm the required colours, activation method and lighting modes for the chosen model.
Cup and decorationCheck serving capacity, print area and artwork visibility with the lighting switched on and off.
Battery and cleaningConfirm battery access, cleaning limits and whether any components must be removed.
ShipmentReview battery details and destination-specific packaging or transport requirements before booking.

Are LED cups waterproof or dishwasher safe?

These properties depend on the cup and light-module design. Request the model-specific cleaning instructions and relevant test information. Do not assume that a light-up cup can be immersed, microwaved or placed in a dishwasher.

Can LED cups with logo share the same power and lighting?

No common configuration is assumed. State the desired lighting, activation and power preference, then review each selected model. The logo should be checked with the light on and off.

Review activation, power and components by LED model

Confirm activation, lighting, power, included components and cleaning for each shortlisted model. A shared battery, seal, replaceable or removable module, or included lighting unit is not assumed across the range. Review decoration positions and methods against the selected surface and artwork.

How should I specify activation and power?

Describe the venue workflow, desired effect and power preference, then ask the team to confirm the exact model configuration and instructions.
