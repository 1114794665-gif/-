Buying Questions

What should I send for a custom coaster quote?

Include the model, container-base dimensions, logo, intended venue, estimated quantity and destination. Confirm material, size, artwork, packing and any LED configuration in the quotation.

Are all silicone coasters absorbent or heat resistant?

Do not assume either property from the material name or image. State the intended use and request evidence or a sample for the selected model and surface.

Can I order coasters in a custom size?

Yes. Tell us your required diameter or length and width, shape, thickness and cup-base dimensions. We customize the size to your project and confirm the drawing, logo area and sample before the order. Coasters are specified by dimensions, not beverage capacity.

How much do custom cups with a logo cost?

A project quote is needed because the model, quantity, decoration, accessories, packing and delivery affect the price. Compare the same specification and ask which setup, sample, tooling and delivery charges are included. External reference prices are not published here as confirmed selling prices.

Can I add my logo to a cup, coaster or opener?

Request a decoration review for the selected model, surface and logo position. The artwork and finished appearance should be approved before bulk production. Provide the design, intended size and colour references. Do not assume that a marking method suitable for one material will work on every product.

Can I request samples before a bulk order?

Yes. Discuss an existing model sample, a decorated sample or a pre-production sample for the selected project. Confirm the sample specification, charges, preparation time, freight and any credit terms in writing. A digital proof checks layout and does not replace physical sample approval.

Can I mix product models, colours or designs in one order?

Ask for a quote that lists each model, colour, design and quantity separately. Mixing options depend on the project and production arrangement. Do not assume that a combined total meets every model’s minimum or that multiple designs share the same setup charge.
