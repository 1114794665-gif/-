Logo Customization and Samples

For wholesale LED light-up PS + ABS ice buckets, use this model as the starting point for logo and packing discussions. Artwork and colours shown are references. We will help you confirm the model configuration, suitable decoration, artwork placement and sample approach before ordering.

How the lighting colour works (every LED model): small runs ship in a fixed single colour — white or yellow. From 100 pcs the seven-colour setup is available: red, orange, yellow, green, white, blue and purple, switched on the unit, no extra parts, so one bucket covers a whole colour theme. The LEDs are RGB, so in bulk a custom shade (cyan, pink, amber and so on) can be mixed to your colour reference.
