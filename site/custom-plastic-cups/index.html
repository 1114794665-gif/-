PROCESS FIT / BY BODY TYPE

Which decoration fits which cup

Ten body types, seven processes. Solid means we recommend it for that shape — best cost, best yield. Outline means it can be done, with the caveat in the last column. Anything not listed does not hold up in use, so we will not quote it.

RecommendedPossible, with a caveat
Body type
Decoration that fits

What decides it

Beer & pint cupsPP · PS · PC · Tritan
Screen printHeat transferColour-change inkUV printHydro dip

The straight taper takes a screen cleanly. PP goes through a flame pass first — skip it and the ink lifts with a thumb.

Handled mugs & steinsPC · PP
Screen printHydro dipUV printColour-change ink

The handle blocks the screen, so print lands either side of it. For a full wraparound, hydro dip is the only route.

Stemmed wine, champagne & cocktailAcrylic · PS · PET · Tritan
Screen printLaser engravingUV printHeat transferColour-change ink

A laser on acrylic leaves a frosty white mark — the cleanest logo these shapes take. Print goes on the bowl, never the stem.

Shot & shooter glassesPS · Acrylic · PP
Screen printUV printLaser engravingColour-change ink

Short body: there is no room for a full wrap. One colour or a small mark works better than a big graphic.

Tumblers & double-wall straw cupsAcrylic · PP · Tritan · PC
Heat transferUV printScreen printLaser engraving

A full wrap is heat transfer work. On a double wall only the outside can be printed — never between the walls.

Shaped cups (bat, fishbowl, sphere)PP · PS · Acrylic
Hydro dipUV printColour-change ink

The curvature is too tight for a screen or a transfer. Only hydro dipping wraps the whole body.

Colour-change cups (cold or heat)PP · PS
Colour-change inkScreen print (base)UV print

A white base goes down first, then the thermochromic layer, and both are cured. A plain moulded body cannot change colour — it needs printed area.

Silicone cups (pint, stein, barrel)Silicone · compression moulded
Colour in the materialMoulded-in 3D logoSilicone screen / pad print

Silicone takes its colour in the material, not on the surface. Printing needs a silicone-specific ink and a second heat cure.

Collapsible silicone cupsSilicone + nylon ring
Colour in the materialSilicone pad print (away from folds)

Ink on a fold cracks after a couple of collapses. Print only on the flat band of the body.

Silicone tumblers & shot cupsSilicone, with lid and straw
Colour in the materialMoulded-in 3D logoSilicone screen print

Lids and straws can be moulded in a second colour to make a set. Small parts take no large print — keep the mark small and simple.

Every body type on this page is our own mould, moulded and decorated in our own plant — so the entry is 10 pcs, plain or printed, one colour or many. Screens and ink matching are charged as setup on top of the unit price, never as a minimum order. See the four order tiers →

Plastic water bottles, handled tumblers and travel cups

Tritan and PP water bottles, handled tumblers and travel cups are produced in the same injection and printing lines as our stadium cups. Capacities run from 16 oz to 32 oz, with Tritan for clarity and impact resistance, PP for lightweight programmes and acrylic for a glass-like finish. Logos are applied by screen printing or UV printing depending on the surface and artwork; laser marking is used where the material allows it. Send the model reference and quantity so we can confirm capacity, lid and straw fit before sampling.

Blank drinkware for resellers, sublimation and decal programmes

Blank and unbranded cups are supplied for resellers, Etsy sellers and decorators who apply their own artwork. Options include blank tumblers, blank can coolers and blank pint cups in Tritan, PP and acrylic, plus UV DTF cup wraps and decals for customers who prefer a wrap rather than direct printing. Private label and white label packing are available. Tell us the decoration method you use and we will confirm a compatible surface.

Colour-changing, mood and glow cups

Colour-changing and mood cups react to cold liquid, which makes them a repeat order item for bars, resorts and event promoters. Glow and LED effects are covered on the LED drinkware page. Colour change is applied over a plastic body, so the base model, capacity and print method are confirmed together before sampling.

Do you supply blank cups for sublimation or resale?

Blank tumblers, can coolers and pint cups are supplied for resellers and decorators. Tell us which decoration method you use — heat transfer, UV DTF wrap or direct print — and we confirm a compatible surface before you order.

Can you print Tritan water bottles and handled tumblers?

Tritan bottles and handled tumblers are printed by screen or UV printing depending on the surface and artwork. Send the model reference and quantity and we confirm the method and print area.

Are colour-changing cups available in bulk?

Colour-changing and mood cups are produced in bulk over plastic bodies. We will help you confirm the base model and capacity first, because the colour-change layer is applied to a specific body.

Questions Before You Order

What are your payment terms?

We require a 30% deposit. The remaining 70% must be paid in full before shipment.

Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept T/T (bank transfer), Western Union and Alipay.

Which currencies can I pay in?

We accept USD, CNY, EUR and GBP. Other currencies can also be arranged for your order.

Which trade terms do you offer?

We offer EXW (Ex Works), FOB Shenzhen, C&F and DDP. The trade term is agreed with you for each order.

Can you develop a new cup shape that is not in your product list?

Yes. Our OEM/ODM service develops a plastic cup from your idea, sketch or reference. Start with a free design proposal and visual mockup, then progress through structure and tooling, sample approval and production.

Should I choose the cup capacity before sending my logo?

Send your required capacity and the cup style or reference together with your logo. We use that selected model to prepare the artwork layout and product mockup.
