THREE ROUTES · CHOOSE BY WHAT YOU CHANGE

Not every project needs a new mold.

Most briefs start from a model that is already in production. Change the decoration only and no new mold is needed; change the shape and it has to be assessed. Pick the route by what you want to change, not by what the project is called. Each route carries its own minimum order; it is printed on the card.

BEFORE THE NUMBER

What decides the price and the schedule.

Two enquiries that look the same can come back with different numbers. These four points are usually what moves them.

  • 01 · ToolingA new mold for a body, lid or component is a one-off cost with its own schedule. Reusing an existing mold takes it out of the quotation entirely.
  • 02 · Part count and fitMore parts mean more assembly, more fit checks and more ways for a first sample to come back wrong.
  • 03 · Decoration and finishNumber of print positions, colour count, coverage, laser area, coating and multi-side artwork each add setup and inspection steps.
  • 04 · Sampling rounds and approvalsEvery extra revision round adds time. Approving artwork, colour and sample version together is the fastest route.

TYPICAL SCHEDULE

1–2 daysExisting sample
5–7 daysCustomised sample
30–40 daysNew tooling, drawing to first sample
25–30 daysMass production, standard run
Sampling
A logo-only sample usually ships the day after artwork is confirmed. A fully customised sample — colour matching, a new structure or a multi-part set — takes 5–7 days.
New tooling
30–40 days end to end. Machining one version of the mold is about 3–4 weeks of that — the rest is scheduling, trial shots and your approval on the first sample.
Production
25–30 days for a standard run. Custom work runs longer: colour matching, special inks, multi-part sets and a first batch off a brand-new mold all add time on top. Inside a standard run the moulding stage is 5–10 days for Tritan and silicone cups, around 20 days for the LED range.

The figures for your project are written into the quotation, including the date the schedule starts and what would change it.

BEFORE YOU REQUEST A QUOTE

Start with what you know.

Can I start with 10 pieces?

Small-batch customization starts from 10 pieces on eligible configurations. Share the model, number of designs and packaging requirements; custom tooling and special specifications are reviewed separately.

Does one-day sampling apply to a new mold?

Sampling can be as fast as one day for eligible projects. New molds, new structures and complex decoration require a separate assessment. Confirm sample type, artwork approval and timing in the quote.

What does 7–10 day door-to-door delivery include?

The 7–10 days is transit only, counted from the day your goods are packed and handed to the carrier. Production is not included: a standard run takes 25–30 days ex-works, and new tooling takes 30–40 days end to end. Plan production and shipping as two separate blocks when you set a launch date.

Can I enquire without a model or drawing?

Yes. Describe the intended use, approximate quantity and required arrival date. You can add a reference image or artwork on the existing enquiry form.

Eight details that make the first quote usable.
